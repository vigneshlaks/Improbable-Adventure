# Summary: 2026-10-16

## Changes
- **Bots can now play whole runs.** `pnpm sim runs --bot greedy --n 300` plays complete acts (five Minions, a rest stop, an Elite, the Boss) and reports the win rate, where runs end, win rates by enemy tier, which structures winning runs used, and whether each of the spec's balance targets is met. Rewards and the rest stop are handled by a simple "taste" for each bot.
- **First numbers: the act is far too hard for everything but the Oracle.** Over 300 runs the greedy bot won 1.3% (target 55 to 70%), the prober and cautious bots won none, the random bot won none (target under 5%, met) and the Oracle won all of them (target over 95%, met). The wall is the Boss (the greedy bot wins 3% of Boss fights, 62% of Elite fights and 92% of Minion fights).
- **Which numbers would fix it.** A sweep with the greedy bot showed enemy HP is the strongest lever (Elite and Boss HP at a quarter lifts it from 1% to 37%), then the Certainty pool (200 instead of 100 gives 20%); extra turns do not help because runs are lost to Certainty, and a bigger rest heal alone does little. No single change reaches 55%, so it will take a combination. Nothing was changed yet.
- Tests were written first and checked by breaking the code on purpose. 539 tests pass.
