# 2026-10-20

## A picture screen with keys
The page now opens on a picture of the fight instead of text: the enemy on the left, the stream and its question in the middle, your structures on the right, a row along the top showing the act and your Certainty, and the keys you can press along the bottom. Everything can be played with the keyboard, the run overview opens with `O`, and `T` switches to the old text screen, where every command still works.

## Red theme
The page now uses a red color scheme in both the light and dark versions.
