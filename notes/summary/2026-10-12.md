# Summary: 2026-10-12

## Changes
- **Challenges.** The enemy can now ask each turn's question: Presence (which of 12 values are really in the stream), Frequency (name the heaviest live value), or Count (how many distinct values are live). Presence decoys are deleted values (ghosts) or values the enemy can never emit.
- **Damage rules.** The three formulas from the spec: Frequency (blocked below the true count, wasted force beyond it), Count (damage falls off across a tolerance band, a miss costs Certainty) and Presence (damage per real target, a penalty per decoy). Damage is rounded to six decimals so saved fights do not depend on floating-point noise.
- **Grading.** Strikes are judged against the exact live stream, never against what a player's structures reported.
- 37 new tests for challenges and damage. Mutation checks confirmed the tests catch an off-by-one at the block boundary, missing ghost decoys, and a one-sided Count error.
- **The fight state machine.** A fight now runs end to end: deploy structures, ingest the enemy's burst, face a challenge, strike, take the enemy's attack, and repeat until the enemy's HP or the player's Certainty runs out, or the turn limit passes. The fight state is plain data that survives JSON and is never edited in place, so a fight replays identically from its seed.
- `observe` shows only what a player may see (the true answers stay hidden), `legalActions` lists representative moves that are always accepted, and `groundTruth` gives bots and tests the real answers.
- 22 scripted-fight tests; 274 tests pass in total and lint is clean. A survey of 300 seeds with a greedy script won every fight, with no errors.

## Known limitations
- LogLog reports 95% bounds on tiny streams where its error law does not hold.
- Linear Counting's infinite upper bound has no JSON encoding yet (answers and events, not snapshots).
- Floating-point determinism across JavaScript engines is partly addressed (damage rounding, exact exponents); the Zipf sampler still uses `exp` and `log`.
- The fight's numbers (enemy HP, attack, size, turn limit) are supplied by the caller and are not in `balance.json` yet.
- CI has not run yet.
