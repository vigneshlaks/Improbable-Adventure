# Summary: 2026-10-14

## Changes
- **Bloom Filter and Reservoir Sampling now keep honest ranges when the enemy deletes values.** Both ignore deletions, so they kept counting deleted values, and in play their ranges often missed the live truth (Bloom's held the truth 100, 51 and 29 percent of the time with no, some and many deletions). They now count the deletions they ignore and widen the low bound of their ranges accordingly. Measured in play, all of their ranges now hold the truth at or above the 95 percent claimed.
- **The cost is a vaguer low bound.** Under real deletions the low bound drops to 0 (Bloom's range reads "0 to however many it flagged"). The high bound is unchanged, and it is the bound a player strikes at to avoid being blocked.
- The decision is recorded in `SPEC.md`.
- 11 new tests, written first and failing on the old code, plus checks that break the fix on purpose; 480 tests pass and lint is clean.

## Known limitations
- Linear Counting and LogLog ignore deletions too and were not changed. In play their ranges still miss the truth under deletions, and LogLog's also misses on small streams.
- Tier sizes, HP, attack and turn limits are placeholders; Bosses are almost unbeatable without perfect answers.
- The starting loadout has no Count-Min.
- Linear Counting's infinite upper bound has no JSON encoding yet.
- CI has not run yet.
