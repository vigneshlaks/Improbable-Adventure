# Summary: 2026-10-14

## Changes
- **Bloom Filter and Reservoir Sampling now keep honest ranges when the enemy deletes values.** Both ignore deletions, so they kept counting deleted values, and in play their ranges often missed the live truth (Bloom's held the truth 100, 51 and 29 percent of the time with no, some and many deletions). They now count the deletions they ignore and widen their ranges accordingly. Measured in play, all of their ranges now hold the truth at or above the 95 percent claimed.
- **Reservoir's Frequency and Count ranges stay useful.** A first, blunt widening left them with a low bound of zero. The structures know the share of inserted units that have been deleted, so Reservoir now scales its Frequency estimate to the live share and bounds the ghosts in its Count with a binomial. Its low bound is back: Count's is never zero, and Frequency's is zero in a fraction of answers (12 to 61 percent with deletions) instead of all of them. On the game's own streams, including growing bursts and drifting favorites, the ranges hold the truth 99 to 100 percent of the time.
- **Presence stays conservative.** For Bloom and Reservoir Presence the low bound is still usually 0 under deletions, because how many ghosts land among the targets depends on how the challenge chose them, not on how many ghosts exist. A model of ghosts held the truth in only 57 percent of challenges at light deletions, so it was rejected. The high bound stays informative.
- The decision is recorded in `SPEC.md`.
- **Linear Counting and LogLog got the same treatment.** They count the insertions and deletions they see and lower their low bound by the ghosts that the deleted share allows (never more than the number of deletions). A shared helper does the arithmetic. Measured in play, Linear Counting's range now holds the truth 94 to 100 percent of the time (it was 96, then 0 and 0 with deletions), with a low bound that is never zero. The price is width: its range grows from about 34 values wide with no deletions to about 280 with many.
- **Two things this does not fix.** The estimate (the middle of the range) is still the count of values ever seen, so under deletions it sits above the live count (Linear Counting: 11 percent above at medium deletions, 67 percent at high). And LogLog's range still misses on small streams even with no deletions (71 to 85 percent in play), which is its known weakness; the HyperLogLog fusion is its remedy.
- New tests, written first and failing on the old code, plus checks that break the fix on purpose, including tests that run the enemy generator under harsh trait mixes; 546 tests pass and lint is clean.

## Known limitations
- The Count estimate is biased high under deletions, and LogLog's range misses on small streams (see above).
- Tier sizes, HP, attack and turn limits are placeholders; Bosses are almost unbeatable without perfect answers.
- The starting loadout has no Count-Min.
- Linear Counting's infinite upper bound has no JSON encoding yet.
- CI has not run yet.
