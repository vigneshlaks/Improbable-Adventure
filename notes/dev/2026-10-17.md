## Tuning the act
- **Plan** (from the last entry): Elite and Boss HP at a quarter plus Certainty 150. Measured first (Greedy, 150 runs): that gave 42% (Elite 84%, Boss 70%), short of 55%. Minions capped it: 93% per fight is 70% over five.
- **Search** (Greedy / Prober / Cautious / Random run win %, 150 to 200 runs): HP 40/22/50 C150 42/14/8/0; HP 20/30/50 C150 **51**/31/11/1 (N=150; 47 on the 200 seeds below); HP 15/30/50 52/35/12/3; HP 20/35/60 49/27/10/1; HP 20/40/70 49/25/9/1; HP 25/40/70 45/21/8/0. Tier HP ordering matters: the first plan would have made Elites (22 HP) easier than Minions (40).
- **Why more tuning does not help.** Run win % did not move across Certainty 150/175/200, Boss attack 6/7/9 or Elite attack 5/6 (all 51); that meant the fights still lost were not lost to Certainty. A new `lostToTurnLimit` count per tier (added to the run report, written first) showed every lost Minion and Elite fight was a turn-limit loss. Turn limits +2/+4 gave 47 to 49. OVERSHOOT_EXP 1 / 0.8 / 0.65 / 0.5 gave 47 / 49 / 50 / 49.
- **Cause.** Greedy fight win % by weak point with a mid-run kit (exact, reservoir, bloom, HyperLogLog, Count-Min; 150 seeds): Minion presence 100 / frequency 91 / count 99; Elite 100 / 77 / 94; Boss 100 / 84 / 85. Frequency is the weak point, always lost to the turn limit: Greedy cannot find the shielded value's count (Exact Memory's gamble, Count-Min's overshoot) and its strikes deal little.
- **Applied**: `STARTING_CERTAINTY` 150, tier HP 20 / 30 / 50 (Minion / Elite / Boss). Nothing else in `balance.json` changed.

## Changes to the tool
- **Pooled targets.** The opener and structure targets were checked per bot, which could never be met: the Prober opens with Reservoir Sampling by definition and Greedy never deploys it. The spec says "Greedy-family" runs and states the structure target for winning runs generally, so both are now pooled over the winning runs of Greedy, Prober and Cautious (each in proportion to its wins; the Oracle and Random are excluded). Target ids are now `family-opener` and `family-structures`.
- **Lost to the turn limit** per tier in the run report (`RunFightRecord.reason`).
- **Guard test.** A sim test requires Oracle over 95%, Greedy over 30% and Random under 5% over 60 runs, well away from the measured numbers, to catch a repeat of the 1.3%.
- Existing tests that had the old numbers baked in (Minion HP 40, Certainty 100: Oracle wins a Minion on turn 2 now, not 3) derive them from `balance.json`.
- 546 tests pass (7 more than the 539 at the end of the last entry). Mutation checks on the pooled targets: 9 breaks, all caught after adding tests for the Oracle being excluded, the 15% boundary and the no-family case.
