## M4 step 3c: Fading (the Stable Bloom Filter)
- **Scope**: Bloom Filter only. On Count-Min, decay would push the estimate under the true count (breaking "never underestimates" and the Frequency damage rules); not built.
- **Structure** (`stableBloomFilter.ts`): 3-bit cells (`FADING_CELL_BITS`), cells `floor(Space / 3)`, `k` from the Bloom optimum, decay rate from the paper's stable point, decay positions from a hash of the insert number (replays exactly; no RNG state to snapshot). The factory and `restoreStructure` build it for `kind: bloomFilter` with the `fading` enchantment, so the kind, the snapshot header and the UI name ("Stable Bloom Filter" from the content) all follow.
- **A bug the test caught in my own bound**: I first wrote the chance a value is forgotten as `p^k` (every cell decayed). A Bloom-style test needs all `k` cells non-zero, so a value is forgotten when any one has decayed: `1 - (1 - p)^k`. The age test found observed 25% where the first formula said one in a billion. I checked that the decay positions are Poisson as modelled and that the filter's cells behave per the model before concluding the bound, not the filter, was wrong.
- **Range**: false negatives mean the high bound widens for the first time on any Presence structure: `high = min(targets, flagged + forgotten)`, the low side as for Bloom, each side at 97.5%.
- **Tests** (one per topic; 5 tests): parameters, factory and the paper's rate (28.6 for m = 1000, k = 3, n = 100, Max = 3, and a small-filter case where the 1/cells term matters); behaviour (flagged, forgotten, deletes counted, ghosts lower the low bound, clone, merge by maximum, snapshot, replay); stable fullness and false-positive rate after 2,000 inserts against a plain Bloom Filter that saturates; the forgetting rate at five ages against the bound; the range's coverage, its upward widening, and the exact shared-budget quantiles. The generic snapshot tests also run on the new variant. Mutation checks: 22 breaks; seven survivors (the 1/cells term, ghosts in the low bound, the shared budget on each side, the merge direction, the rebuilt fullness) each got an assertion.
- **Does it help? No.** Greedy, Elite, Presence-forced, 150 seeds, plain Bloom vs Fading: default traits 100% vs 0%; High Churn 100% vs 14%; High Growth 100% vs 0%; both 100% vs 5%. Presence targets are drawn from all live values and the default window is about `n` inserts, so most real targets have faded. Whole runs (300): Greedy 47.0%, Prober 28.7%, Cautious 8.7%, Random 0.7%, Oracle 100%; same four targets met. The bots never take Fading. Kept as a reward (the spec lists it; it can simply not be attached) but flagged as a trap at these Space sizes.
- **Not done**: Light, Merge.

## Frequency gap: diagnosis recorded, deferred to the Phase 2 structures
Watching four lost Elite Frequency fights (Greedy, mid-run kit: Exact Memory, Reservoir, Bloom, HyperLogLog, Count-Min; seeds 500 to 518), every loss is the Count-Min Sketch's estimate being far above the truth, and Greedy striking on it:
- True counts were 1 to 11; the sketch said 7, 18, 26, 32, 39, 48 (true 1 to 2), 51, 93, 139, 341, 1,298 (true 3 to 11), growing with the stream. Damage per strike was 0.1 to 3 of the 20 a perfect strike deals, so the fight ends at the turn limit with the enemy nearly full.
- The cause is the sketch's width. Bots give it 4,096 bits; at 32 bits per counter and depth 4 that is 32 columns, so each counter holds about N / 32 of the stream (about 47 for 1,500 events). The documented bound, an overshoot of up to (e / w) x N, is honest and useless here, and the range's low bound is 0.
- Greedy's blind strike of 1 (before any structure is deployed) deals the full 20 when the count is 1, which is common on flat streams (skew Low).
- Candidate remedies, **not measured** (the experiment was stopped by the user): narrower counters (the spec fixes 32 x w x d), a larger Space share or lower depth for the sketch, a Count-Mean-Min point estimate (departs from "never underestimates"), a prior from the visible traits (the bots cannot see the stream size), or smaller streams relative to Space.
- **Decision (user, 2026-10-17):** keep the gap documented and revisit it with the Phase 2 structures (Count Sketch, Top-K Tracker and the other Frequency tools), not with the MVP ones.
