- **Design departures from the spec's sketch** (all in `SPEC.md`, "Enchantments, step 3a"): an enchantment attaches to an **owned structure kind**, not a "slot" (nothing is deployed at a rest stop), so the action is `enchant { enchantmentId, kind }`; one enchantment per structure, attaching another returns the first to the pack, an attached one can be moved; fusing returns the inputs' enchantments; enchantments are rewards and go to a pack.

## Frequency gap: diagnosis recorded, deferred to the Phase 2 structures
Watching four lost Elite Frequency fights (Greedy, mid-run kit: Exact Memory, Reservoir, Bloom, HyperLogLog, Count-Min; seeds 500 to 518), every loss is the Count-Min Sketch's estimate being far above the truth, and Greedy striking on it:
- True counts were 1 to 11; the sketch said 7, 18, 26, 32, 39, 48 (true 1 to 2), 51, 93, 139, 341, 1,298 (true 3 to 11), growing with the stream. Damage per strike was 0.1 to 3 of the 20 a perfect strike deals, so the fight ends at the turn limit with the enemy nearly full.
- The cause is the sketch's width. Bots give it 4,096 bits; at 32 bits per counter and depth 4 that is 32 columns, so each counter holds about N / 32 of the stream (about 47 for 1,500 events). The documented bound, an overshoot of up to (e / w) x N, is honest and useless here, and the range's low bound is 0.
- Greedy's blind strike of 1 (before any structure is deployed) deals the full 20 when the count is 1, which is common on flat streams (skew Low).
- Candidate remedies, **not measured** (the experiment was stopped by the user): narrower counters (the spec fixes 32 x w x d), a larger Space share or lower depth for the sketch, a Count-Mean-Min point estimate (departs from "never underestimates"), a prior from the visible traits (the bots cannot see the stream size), or smaller streams relative to Space.
- **Decision (user, 2026-10-17):** keep the gap documented and revisit it with the Phase 2 structures (Count Sketch, Top-K Tracker and the other Frequency tools), not with the MVP ones.
