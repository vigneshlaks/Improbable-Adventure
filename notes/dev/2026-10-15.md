## Frequency tuning
**Diagnosis** (Minion, Frequency forced, 150 seeds, shipped loadout). Greedy strikes Exact Memory's figure. When the shielded value is in Exact Memory's table (128 values at 8,192 bits) the strike is exact and deals the full BASE; when it is not, the strike is blocked. Blocked strikes were 57% of Greedy's Frequency strikes, and mean f/s on the others was 1.00. Frequency is a gamble on what Exact Memory happened to see. With Count-Min Sketch added (5 owned), blocks fell to 3% but the sketch overshoots about 3x on these streams (f/s of 0.29 to 0.40), and with `OVERSHOOT_EXP` 2 that cuts damage to about a ninth: Greedy won 27% against 77% by the gamble.

**Sweep** (120 seeds per cell, Minion Frequency win % for Greedy / Prober / Cautious / Random; then natural Minion and Elite means over the first three):

**Change.** `OVERSHOOT_EXP` 2 to 1 (damage = BASE x f / s). With the shipped loadout nothing moves (it never owns the sketch). With Count-Min the sketch route is as good as the Exact Memory gamble for Greedy and still rewards skill (Prober 38, Cautious 47). Below 1 Frequency becomes nearly trivial with the sketch (Prober and Cautious about 78%, Random up to 16%), so 1 was chosen. Recorded in `SPEC.md` ("Frequency falloff").

**Tests** (written first, failing): `balance.test.ts` pins the value; a sim test has Greedy play 60 Frequency Minions with Count-Min owned and requires more than 55% wins (it measured 32% at exponent 2). Putting 2 back fails it.

**Check on the shipped config** (200 fights per bot, seeds 7000 to 7199): Minion Greedy 88%, Prober 80.5%, Cautious 56.5%, Random 25%; Elite Greedy 63.5%, Prober 33.5%, Cautious 24%, Random 7%. In line with the 14th within seed noise.

**Not changed, and why.**
- The shipped loadout's Frequency is still the Exact Memory gamble (Cautious 3 to 9%, because it strikes Reservoir's wide upper bound). That follows from Reservoir's honest bounds and the loadout, not from a balance number.
- `BLOCK_PENALTY` was not varied; Greedy's wrong-strike share on Minions is 38%, above the spec's 10 to 30% target, and mostly comes from that gamble. Worth revisiting once the sketch is a reward in the run loop (M4).
- Count-Min's own estimate (its minimum row, always at or above the truth) is unchanged.

## Run loop, step 1 (M4)
M4 is split into: (1) run state machine with rewards, upgrades and the rest stop; (2) HyperLogLog and the LogLog + Linear Counting fusion; (3) enchantments and transforms; (4) `sim runs` with bots playing whole runs, checked against the spec's balance targets; (5) run mode in the terminal game. This entry is step 1.

- **Engine** (`packages/engine/src/run`): `RunState` wraps a `FightState` (JSON-safe, copy on write) with phase `fight | reward | rest | won | lost`, Certainty and its maximum, owned structures, upgrade counts, the offered reward ids, a reward RNG state, and turns used. `RunConfig` is plain data (rules, encounters, rest points, reward pool, upgrade effects), so the engine stays free of content. Names exported at package level: `createRun`, `stepRun`, `legalRunActions`, `observeRun`, `turnsTaken` (`step` and `legalActions` already belong to the fight). `RunAction` adds `chooseReward` and `leaveRest` to the fight's actions without touching `Action`.
- **Fight rules** gained an optional `damageMultiplier` (default 1) for Power; the strike result carries the scaled damage.
- **Content**: `balance.json` gained `RUN_ENCOUNTERS`, `REST_BEFORE`, `REWARD_CHOICES`, `REWARD_WEIGHTS`; new `upgrades.json` (validated, strict); `buildRunConfig(seed)` designs encounter `i` from `<seed>/encounter/<i>`; `fightRules` is now shared with `buildFightConfig`.
- **Decisions** (recorded in `SPEC.md`, "Run loop, step 1"): rest stop before the Elite; Certainty carries; rewards after every fight but the last; owned structures not re-offered; Resolve also raises current Certainty; Recovery applies after each won fight, capped.
- **Tests** (written first): 20 engine run tests, 1 fight test for the multiplier, 9 content tests (incl. a whole act won with perfect strikes on the real content, through rewards and the rest stop). 30 new tests; 474 pass, lint clean. Mutation checks: 16 on the engine (all caught after adding a test that each encounter has its own stream), 6 on the content builder (the survivor, encounters sharing a seed, got a test).
- **Not done:** HyperLogLog, fusion, enchantments, bots playing runs (so no balance numbers for the act yet), run mode in the terminal.
