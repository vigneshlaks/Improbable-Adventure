## M8 RL bridge
- `packages/env-server` (TypeScript) and `rl/` (Python) as described in SPEC.md (RL bridge, "What was built" and "Result"). 222 repo tests pass; the Python tests (client 2, Gymnasium and vector 2) pass in `rl/.venv` (Python 3.9, gymnasium 1.1.1, stable-baselines3 and sb3-contrib 2.7.1, torch 2.8.0, installed with the user's approval).
- Bugs the tests found early: `strikeSubset` takes target positions, not values; the engine's step reward omits the run-loss cost for a fight lost to the turn limit (kept as a documented difference); the per-turn cost belongs on `enemyAttacked`, not on `turnStarted` or `burstIngested`.
- Mask building called `observeRun` once per action; computing it once took the environment from about 960 to about 5,400 steps per second.
- Mutation checks: strike meanings, mask legality, deploy rounding, reward weights, observation scaling, the step limit and the cut-off penalty are caught. Swapping the enemy-defeated and run-loss weights survived at first (both 1), so a reward test with distinct weights was added.
- Training history (all on held-out seeds 100000 to 100199 unless stated; Greedy 54.0 percent):
  - 500k steps: 6.0 percent (12 of 200); random 0.5 percent. About 120 policy updates, undertrained.
  - 4M steps, four runs that ended early with `Expected parameter probs ... Simplex()` at about 2.1M to 2.4M steps. I first suspected an all-masked state (700 games scanned, none), a non-finite reward or observation (500 more games and 30,000 resets, none), divergence (target KL, learning-rate decay: no change), and a non-finite gradient (a pre-step hook never fired). Finally captured the failing inputs from the exception's stack: weights, observation and mask were fine, and the probabilities were ordinary with row sums off by about 1e-6. The check is the cause; see SPEC.
  - A probe of a checkpoint (1,500 games, no training) showed 1,490 steps per game: the policy stalled until the cut-off because a cut-off cost nothing. Fixed by charging the run-loss weight and lowering `RL_MAX_STEPS` to 400 (the longest recorded run is 105 actions). Reward then climbed (mean 4.7 to 6.0 per run, episode length 341 to 94).
  - Final run (fresh, 4M steps, validation off): 186 of 200 (93.0 percent) on 100000 to 100199 and 186 of 200 on 500000 to 500199 (Greedy 108 and 97). 188 and 186 finished games saved as replays through the new `replay` command and all verify. The rest were cut off at 400 steps (12 and 14), 2 lost in the first set.
- Added along the way: `PdaVecEnv` checks every new state for no legal action, a non-finite observation or a non-finite reward and writes `rl/models/bad-state.json` with the seed and actions (never triggered); `Env` records through `RunRecorder`; `evaluate(..., replay_dir=)` and `train --evaluate ... --seed-base N --save-replays DIR`.
- Not done: behavior cloning from replays (the spec's optional pretraining; not needed to reach the bar), more than one training seed, a speed comparison against the Oracle, committing a trained model, and any CI job that trains (the `env-server` job runs the client tests only).
