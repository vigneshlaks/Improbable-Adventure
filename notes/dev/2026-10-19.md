## M9 step 5: the Cunning enemy
- Engine: `enemies/cunning.ts` (`publicModel`, `craftInserts`, `craftDecoys`), `probe()` on Count-Min, LogLog and HyperLogLog, `FightState.cunningCursor`, `FightRules.cunningSearch`, `StreamTraits.cunning`, `TraitTables.cunning` (`events`, `decoys`), `ChallengeContext.cunning` (crafted Presence decoys). The fight adds the crafted inserts to the burst after the burst itself is drawn (so the enemy's random stream is not shifted) and offers crafted decoys at a Presence challenge. Content: `traits.json` (`cunning` levels), narration, `enchantments.json` (Seeded `reward: true`). Bots: take Seeded as a reward and attach it. Layout +1 observation number. Definitions and numbers in SPEC.md ("Phase 2, step 5").
- Design: the attacker rebuilds each deployed structure from its snapshot with the secret seed removed. That is the literal reading of "keeps attacking with the public seed", and it needs no per-structure attack code that could read a seed: Seeded works because the state is read with the wrong hashes. Bloom and Cuckoo are attacked through the public `query`, in chunks of candidates.
- Tests (`cunning.test.ts`, `fight.test.ts`, `challenges.test.ts`): the inflation against open and secret seeds for each structure, the enemy blind to the secret (same state, different secrets, same values), the candidate budget, nothing to aim at, sharing between structures, the rarest-first order of the register attack, the fight's burst size and cursor, JSON-safety, and the share of crafted decoys. Mutation checks, 16: 9 caught at once, 6 survived and led to tests (register order and tie edge, duplicates between two structures, rounding of the crafted count, the Count-Min core, the share of crafted decoys); the rest are equivalent: skipping the core in the candidates (they are outside the universe, so the guard was dead code and I removed it), crafting decoys for a challenge that is not Presence (only costs time), a loop bound that stops one decoy early (only matters at a chunk edge), and a no-op copy of the snapshots.
- **Found by the Oracle failing a run** (99 percent instead of 100): an Elite whose only weak point was Likeness, with Medium Variety. Its live set held about 3,500 values, above the 1,000 the game allows, so no challenge was ever issued, the Oracle could not strike and lost to the turn limit. Likeness is now a weak point only for Low Variety enemies (weight 0 otherwise), tested in `traits.test.ts` and `enemies.test.ts`. The trait Likeness can still be on any enemy.
- Balance: see SPEC.md. Cunning off, Greedy 43.7 and Prober 31.0; on with Seeded available 37.0 and 24.0.
- Replays re-recorded (`greedy-3-won`, `prober-3-won`: the seeds that still win).
- Not done: a Count Sketch or Top-K attack (the spec lists only three structures), a test of Cunning in the learning environment, exporter templates for Phase 2 structures, retraining the learned agent, SimHash, the Likeness fusions, Merge.
