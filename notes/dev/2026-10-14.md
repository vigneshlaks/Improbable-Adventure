## Delete-aware ranges for Bloom Filter and Reservoir Sampling
**Problem.** A range is a claim about the answer to a challenge, and a challenge asks about values that are live now. Structures that ignore deletes keep flagging or counting deleted values (ghosts), so their ranges missed the live truth. The in-play accuracy report measured it (how often the range held the truth, with no, some and many deletes):

| Structure and question | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bloom, Presence | 100 / 51 / 29% | 100 / 100 / 100% |
| Reservoir, Presence | 100 / 99 / 98% | 100 / 100 / 100% |
| Reservoir, Frequency | 98 / 96 / 90% | 98 / 99 / 100% |
| Reservoir, Count | 99 / 100 / 100% | 99 / 100 / 100% |
| Linear Counting, Count | 96 / 0 / 0% | unchanged |
| LogLog, Count | 74 / 21 / 0% | unchanged |

(One Minion setup with medium variety and skew, one structure with 4,096 bits, about 80 fights per cell.)

**Change.** Each of the two structures now counts the delete events it ignores (`deletes`). Each ghost needs at least one delete, so there are never more ghosts than that count.
- Bloom, Presence: `low = max(0, flagged - collisions - min(d, flagged))`. High is unchanged.
- Reservoir, Presence: the low bound is widened by `min(d, flagged)`. Frequency and Count: the low bound is widened by `d`, never below zero (the sample describes what was ever inserted; live is at least that minus `d`). High bounds are unchanged.
- With no deletes seen, every range is exactly what it was.
- The counter is saved in snapshots (`deletes`), kept by `clone`, and summed by Bloom's `merge`. It is not charged against Space.
- Decision recorded in `SPEC.md` ("Delete-blind ranges").

**Tests (11 new), written first and run against the old code**
- Unit: the counter, the widening arithmetic at `d` of 0, 1, 2, 3 and 50, never below zero, high bound unchanged, and the counter through clone and merge.
- Coverage on a stream with 40 percent deletes, 1,000 seeded trials each, using the engine's own Presence challenge so the ghosts appear as real decoys: Bloom Presence, and Reservoir Presence, Frequency and Count. On the old code the ranges held the truth in 0.3 to 3.7 percent of trials against a required 93 percent. Each test also checks the scenario really does flag ghosts.
- New helpers in `structures/testUtils.ts`: `churnStream` (a stream with deletes and its exact live record) and `presenceTargets`.
- Mutation checks: allowing one ghost too few in Bloom, forgetting to widen Reservoir's Frequency, and losing the count in a snapshot each fail tests.

**Cost.** The fix is conservative. Under any real churn `d` is far larger than the 12 targets, so Bloom's Presence low bound is 0 in 100 percent of answers (the range reads "0 to however many it flagged"), and Reservoir's Presence and Frequency lows are 0 in 100 percent (Count in 38 to 95 percent). The ranges are honest because they say little. The high bound is untouched and is still the bound a player strikes at to avoid being blocked, so Cautious's Frequency strikes are unaffected.
