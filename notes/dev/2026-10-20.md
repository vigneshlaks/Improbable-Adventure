- Open: a typed Space size cannot yet pick a structure's slot; a lost run shows the last fight as "ahead" on the map.

## Red theme
- Colors are variables in `packages/web/src/style.css`; the accent is crimson in both themes, meaning-bearing colors (confirmed green, HP orange, Space rose, tier colors) are kept apart from it. Bar labels use white with a dark outline after the light theme's red fill made dark labels unreadable.
